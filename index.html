SKY Campus is the student program of the Art of Living. This December, it comes to Oakland as a West Coast holiday retreat, at the new center by Lake Merritt.

How to join

Three steps.

01

Pick your path

SKY Campus Happiness if you are new, Sahaj if you are a graduate.

02

03

Watch for the follow-up

Good to know

Before you apply.

Who is this retreat for?

Students from colleges all across the West Coast, every campus, every major, every year. Current university students and incoming students.

I have never meditated. Can I still come?

Yes. Everything is guided step by step, and most students arrive with zero meditation or yoga experience.

Where is it, and when?

Saturday-Sunday, December 19-20, 2026, at the Art of Living center in Oakland. Apply through the form.

How much does it cost?

Students get over 80% scholarship. Spots are limited, so scholarships are limited too, only while spots last.

Can I come with friends from my campus?

Yes, bring them. Each person should submit their own application.

Coming from outside the Bay Area?

If you need accommodation or commute help, the team will work with you to provide some options.
