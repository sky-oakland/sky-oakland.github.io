Inside the retreat

What you'll experience

SKY Campus Happiness is the flagship student program of SKY Campus. It blends ancient breathing practices with modern, practical tools for student life.

SKY Breath Meditation

Learn Sudarshan Kriya™ (SKY) — a powerful, evidence-based breathing technique to release stress, settle the mind, and recharge from within.

Yoga & movement

Gentle, energizing yoga and movement sessions that unwind a semester of desks, screens, and late-night studying.

Emotional intelligence

Practical wisdom for handling pressure, emotions, and relationships — with more clarity, confidence, and ease.

Community & leadership

Connection circles, group processes, and celebration — and a doorway into student-led SKY Campus clubs back on your own campus.
