  1. Go to the registration link

    Go to tinyurl.com/su6yfdxc

  2. Click Register Today, then Register again

    On the course page, click Register Today and then Register again.

  3. Click Login

    On the next page, click Login.

  4. Click Sign up if you do not have an account

    On the login page, click Sign up if you do not have an account yet.

  5. Fill in all the details — use your .edu email here

    Fill in your first name, last name, email address, and password. Use your .edu email here.

  6. After sign up, check the icon to verify

    After successful sign up, check the banner at the top and click verify your student status to get discounted student rates.

  7. You should see this pop up

    You should see: “A verification link has been emailed to you. Please use the link to verify your student email.”

  8. Go to your .edu email and verify your student status

    Open your .edu email, click the verification link, and verify your student status. After it is done, go back to the registration link tinyurl.com/su6yfdxc

  9. You should see the scholarship applied now

    You should see the scholarship applied now. Fill in all the details, enter your payment information, and submit. The total should show $95.00, with the regular tuition of $495 crossed out.

Stuck? Make sure you used your .edu email when you signed up, and check your .edu inbox, including spam, for the verification link. If the scholarship does not show, go back to tinyurl.com/su6yfdxc after verifying.
